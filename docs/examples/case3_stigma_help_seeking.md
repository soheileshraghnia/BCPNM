# Case Study 3: stigma and help-seeking

**Configuration:** `examples/03_stigma_help_seeking.yaml` (intrapersonal construct network;
threshold, case and Boolean rules; ordinal and Boolean states).

**Key results** (paper Section 9; Figure 5):

* High public stigma: only the *stuck* attractor. Moderate: recovered (61.0%), stuck (9.3%),
  intention without action (12.3%), plus synchronous-only cycles. Low: the stuck state still
  exists (4.7%), so lowering public stigma does not release those already stuck (hysteresis).
* From the stuck state: removing self-stigma for one step leads to recovery in 90.9%;
  facilitated contact with care needs at least three steps (~90%); support boosts and
  temporary anti-stigma campaigns help little (≤ 17.8%).
* REVEAL inference from simulated panels (300 or 1,000 persons, 12 waves): precision 1.000,
  recall .889.
