# Case Study 1: the conspiracy of silence

**Configuration:** `examples/01_conspiracy_of_silence.yaml` (five-person family network,
equilibrium Blume–Capel rules, states conceal −1 / silent 0 / acknowledge +1).

**Questions.** Does silence coordinate along ties (the zero–zero term Λ)? Which interventions
reduce the adolescent's silence? Is collective silence path dependent?

**Key results** (paper Section 7; Figure 3):

* Exact three-person model: raising Λ from 0 to 0.8 raises P(all silent) from .051 to .315.
* Family network: the family spends 51.9% of its time in the basin of collective silence;
  without Λ the all-silent attractor does not exist.
* Interventions: a gatekeeper friend raises the adolescent's acknowledgement from .166 to
  .302, but only talk norms (halving Λ) reduce the adolescent's silence (.576 to .441).
* Mean-field hysteresis: silence sets in at δ ≈ −1.64 but lifts only at δ ≈ −1.98.
* Directed network: an opportunity window for A raises acknowledgement by .78 (A), .57 (B),
  .38 (C) and .09 (D, E).
