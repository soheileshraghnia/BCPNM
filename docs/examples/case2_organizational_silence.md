# Case Study 2: organizational silence

**Configuration:** `examples/02_organizational_silence.yaml` (manager, two team leads, four
employees; directed Blume–Capel rules; states endorse status quo −1 / silent 0 / voice +1).

**Key results** (paper Section 8; Figure 4):

* Zero-noise limit: 9 fixed points; 75.2% of initial states end in the *climate of silence*
  (leaders endorse the status quo, employees silent); full voice 1.1%.
* Leverage: a consistently voicing team lead raises the share of outcomes with employee voice
  from 7.5% to 22.0% (manager 13.3%, employee 11.5%).
* Interventions (stationary distribution, β = 1.5): voice training and leader openness raise
  employee voice (.152 to .347 and .385) without reducing silence; psychological safety and
  removing peer coordination reduce silence (.463 to .322 and .345); only the combination
  raises voice (.487) and lowers silence (.322) together.
