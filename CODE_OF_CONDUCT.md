# Code of conduct

We want `bcpnm` to be a welcoming project for researchers and developers of every background
and level of experience.

**Expected behavior.** Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent, give and accept
feedback graciously, credit others' work, and remember that many contributors are learning to
program or learning the methods.

**Unacceptable behavior.** Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Sensitive topics.** Several example models concern suicide, stigma and mental health. Discuss
them with care, avoid stigmatizing language, and do not use the models to make claims about
identifiable individuals.

**Enforcement.** Report concerns to the maintainers (see the repository's contact address).
Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may warn, temporarily restrict or remove
participants who violate this code.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
